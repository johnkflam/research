## 🎯 TOP 3 ENERGY ETFs

### 1. Energy Select Sector SPDR (XLE)
- **Expense Ratio:** 0.08%
- **Holds:** 22 large-cap S&P 500 energy companies
- **AUM:** ~$39 billion (most liquid energy ETF)
- **Yield:** ~2.65%
- **Why:** Pure exposure to the biggest U.S. energy names. Low cost, highly liquid, excludes smaller E&P volatility. Best for core energy exposure.

### 2. Vanguard Energy ETF (VDE)
- **Expense Ratio:** 0.09%
- **Holds:** 106 companies (large/mid/small cap)
- **AUM:** ~$8.2 billion
- **Yield:** ~2.4%
- **Why:** Broader U.S. energy coverage at near-zero cost. Good diversification across market caps. Best for a core energy position.

### 3. iShares Global Energy ETF (IXC)
- **Expense Ratio:** 0.40%
- **Holds:** 51 companies globally (U.S., Europe, Canada)
- **AUM:** ~$2.2 billion
- **Yield:** ~3.48%
- **Why:** International diversification with exposure to global majors (Shell, BP, TotalEnergies). Higher dividend yield. Worth the higher fee for world-wide energy exposure.

## 🏦 TOP 5 ENERGY STOCKS

### 1. Exxon Mobil (XOM)
- **Type:** Integrated major (upstream + downstream + chemical)
- **Why:** Most diversified large cap energy name. Strong Permian Basin presence, battle-tested capital discipline, reasonable valuation vs. peers.

### 2. Chevron (CVX)
- **Type:** Integrated major
- **Yield:** ~4%
- **Why:** Similar profile to Exxon — battle-tested, high-quality blue chip. Strong Permian position + international operations. Solid dividend grower.

### 3. MPLX LP (MPLX)
- **Type:** Master Limited Partnership (midstream infrastructure)
- **Yield:** ~5-6%
- **Why:** Fee-based revenue model (pipelines, storage) — stable regardless of commodity prices. 5-7% annual distribution growth expected. Best for income-focused investors.

### 4. Enterprise Products Partners (EPD)
- **Type:** Midstream MLP
- **Yield:** ~5%
- **Why:** One of the largest publicly traded pipeline companies in North America. Inflation-protected contracts, strong distribution track record, exposure to LNG export growth.

### 5. NextEra Energy (NEE)
- **Type:** Utility + Renewable Energy
- **Why:** World's largest utility/renewable company. Positioned to benefit from surging electricity demand (AI data centers, grid modernization). The "future of energy" play.
