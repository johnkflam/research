# Self Reflection: Interests, Passions & Strengths

**Date:** October 6, 2026  
**Context:** John asked Bob to review his git repository "writings" and identify his interests, passions, and strengths.

---

## 🔥 Your Passions (What Drives You)

**1. Writing & Storytelling**
This is the clearest passion in everything you do. You've been trying to build a consistent writing habit for years, you study writing craft obsessively, and you write about topics ranging from productivity to faith. You explicitly said: *"Why do I write, because I believe that writing is my calling, and writing can change life."*

**2. Helping Others Discover Their Gifts & Purpose**
You write constantly about helping people find their strengths, values, and calling. Your stated mission: *"to help others to discover their gifts and purpose to make the world a better place."* You want to be a life coach and successful author.

**3. Faith & Spiritual Growth**
Your Bible study notes, quiet time journals, and blogs about Jesus show a deep, consistent commitment to your Christian walk. You view faith as the anchor of everything.

**4. Freedom & Autonomy**
You despise the 9-to-5 trap. You've planned extensively for retirement, study financial independence, and dream of living anywhere (warmer climates Jan-Apr). You value freedom to do what you want, where and when you want.

**5. Making a Difference**
You write about legacy, impact, and contribution constantly. *"Making a difference in people's lives"* shows up repeatedly in your goals and reflections.

## 💪 Your Strengths

**1. Systems Thinking & Architecture**
You call this out explicitly: *"I am a system architect, and thinking and synthesizing information are my strengths."* You naturally see patterns, build frameworks, and connect dots.

**2. Writing (when you do it)**
You said it yourself: *"When I write, time goes by fast, and I am energized by it."* You understand writing as thinking and you have a voice.

**3. Learning & Applying Quickly**
Your ultra-learning notes, meta-learning approach, and ability to consume and synthesize huge amounts of information is a genuine strength.

**4. Introspection & Self-Awareness**
The depth of your journaling, values clarification, and reflection shows a rare capacity to examine yourself honestly.

**5. Communication**
You recognize that good writing AND speaking are how you multiply your impact. You study both deliberately.

**6. Consistency (when the habit sticks)**
Your best periods are when you show up daily—morning writing routine, Bible study, planning. You know this about yourself.

## ⚠️ The Tension (What You Know but Keep Fighting)

You already know your weaknesses:

- **Starting vs. Finishing** — *"I would start something enthusiastically, then when it comes to mid-point where it gets hard or boring, I would quit and start something else."*
- **Distraction & shallow work** — Fighting social media, YouTube, TV
- **Evening discipline** — Your biggest time waster
- **Scattered focus** — You want to do too many things at once (RAG, FastHTML, writing, SAAS, ML...)

---

## 🎯 The Synthesis

You're a **reflective practitioner** who wants to **write your way to impact**. Your sweet spot is:

> **Teaching personal development through storytelling + spiritual growth + practical frameworks**

You'd be a great **author, blogger, and life coach** who helps people find their calling, build good habits, and grow spiritually. The AI/tech angle could be a unique differentiator—a platform or tool built by a writer who actually understands technology.

The question you keep asking yourself is *"what should I do in retirement?"* — and the answer is already in your writing. You just need to commit to finishing things instead of starting new ones.
